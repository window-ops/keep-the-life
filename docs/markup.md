# Keep the Life: Markup

- **Chapter tabs:** `div.book-tabs[role=tablist]` at the top of `main`, one `button.tab.ctab` per chapter.
- **Chapter:** `article.chapter#cN`, which contains `header.ch-head > h2` and `div.ch-tabs[role=tablist]`, followed by its sections.
- **Section:** `section.sec#cN-sM`, which contains `h3 > span.sec-n + title + button.plink`.
- **Scene narration:** `div.scene > p`. Narration that comes after the conversation in a section has started keeps the same style as `div.scene.scene-mid`, with the smaller spacing step `--xs` (0.5rem) above and below it.
- **Reply:** `div.ln[data-sp=t|k|b|m|v|s|o|p] > b.sp + p`. The name column is as wide as the longest speaker name, measured by `js/text/names.js`, multiplied by 1.05 and capped at 5.4 rem into `--sp-w`. A continuation of the same reply after a screen is `div.ln.ln-cont` with no `b.sp`. Her label is `b.sp.sp-switch`: a click or Enter switches every one of her labels between SCP-239 and Sigurrós, and the choice is kept. Stage directions are `i.act`. A turn that is only a direction gets `.ln-act`.
- **Site announcement:** `div.ln.ln-pa`, a recording over the speakers, takes no name column and is not indented: it runs the full width of the line, under a small muted caption with a speaker mark and the words "Site announcement", a little smaller in the mono face.
- **Redaction:** `span.redact`.
- **The end:** the book's last scene is `div.scene.scene-final`, centered with no rule, and the last section closes with `div.the-end`, "The end" between two short rules.
- **Screens:** `details.app.app-<kind> > summary.app-cap + div.app-frame`. The kinds are `ghidra`, `ollama`, `konsole`, `vm` (virt-manager with a GNOME guest), and `079`.
- **Whiteboard:** `details.app.app-board`, black marker for the rules, blue for Cicero, red for the modern rhetoric, with the exception drawn as an SVG of dots. No watermark.
- **Translations:** a translation in brackets after foreign words in dialogue is `span.tr`.
- **Printed matter:** every attachment folds. The newspaper (`blockquote.paper`), the AKV poster (`div.ps-wrap > figure.poster`) and the hearing documents sit in `details.app.app-print` with a caption, like the screens. A folded attachment is only its caption, with no padding under it.
- **Hearing documents:** `blockquote.doc` inside `details.app.app-print`. `.doc-call` is a dark video-call tile, holding Sato's whiteboard (`.call-wb`) or Voss's shared screen (`.share`). `.doc-memo` is the Committee's decision on paper. `.doc-note` is Kellerman's notebook: an A5 page (148 mm wide, the pinout page at least 210 mm tall) of paper ruled every 7 mm, with a red margin and one ruled line of space above and below the writing. Everything on it is one hand: Caveat at one size, weight and color, headings underlined, every line written in the middle of the space between two rules. The ruling is an SVG tile, so the page uses no gradients. Sato's whiteboard is seen through her camera with the room around it: wall, the board in its frame with the marker tray, and the desk edge. The writing is one size, the same as on the conference room 3 whiteboard, and the board scrolls sideways on narrow screens. Numbers in tables are left-aligned with their headers. The last child of every block in these documents has no bottom margin.
